yields: "8 servings"
ingredients:
  - "12 lasagna noodles"
  - "1 lb ground beef or Italian sausage"
  - "1 medium onion, diced"
  - "3 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "28 oz crushed tomatoes"
  - "15 oz ricotta cheese"
  - "1 egg"
  - "3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese"
  - "1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese"
  - "1 tsp dried oregano"
  - "Salt and black pepper to taste"
---

### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### 1. Prepare the Meat Sauce
1. In a large saucepan or Dutch oven, brown the ground beef over medium heat until fully cooked. Drain excess fat.
2. Add the diced onion and minced garlic. Sauté for 3-4 minutes until onions are translucent and fragrant.
3. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper.
4. Reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered for 20 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.

#### 2. Prep the Ricotta Mixture
1. In a medium bowl, combine the ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the grated Parmesan cheese.
2. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper, and mix until smooth.

#### 3. Assemble the Lasagna
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Cook the lasagna noodles in boiling salted water according to package instructions, then drain.
3. Spread 1 cup of meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
4. Place a layer of 3 or 4 noodles over the sauce.
5. Spread half of the ricotta mixture over the noodles.
6. Sprinkle with 1 cup of mozzarella cheese and pour 1.5 cups of meat sauce over the top.
7. Repeat the layers (noodles, remaining ricotta, mozzarella, and meat sauce).
8. Finish with a final layer of noodles, the remaining meat sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan cheese.

#### 4. Bake to Golden Perfection
1. Cover the baking dish loosely with aluminum foil (ensuring it doesn't touch the cheese).
2. Bake in the preheated oven for 25 minutes.
3. Remove the foil and bake uncovered for another 25 minutes until the cheese is bubbling and golden brown.
4. Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before slicing and serving.
