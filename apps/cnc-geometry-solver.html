CNC Coordinate Geometry Solver

Blend turning points and sharp corners with precise tangent arcs using vector geometry. Resolves unknowns directly from engineering drawings.

💡 Quick Guide & Legend

1. Point Types

  • Standard Point: Raw path endpoints or start/stop coordinates.
  • Tangent Point: The transition point where a line meets the blend radius.
  • IP Point: The theoretical sharp intersection corner of your path.

2. Resolving Unknowns

Check the Unk checkbox next to a coordinate (Axis 1 or Axis 2) to mark it as unknown. The solver will automatically calculate its position by intersecting the toolpath vector with your fillet circle.

3. Fillet Radii

Specify the blend radius in the Fillet R column of your IP Corner point. The incoming and outgoing lines will blend smoothly using vector bisector geometry.
