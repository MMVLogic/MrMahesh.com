🛡️ Core Safety & Joint Protection Rules (Crucial for 130kg+)

1. Protect Knee & Ankle Joints

Avoid high-impact exercises (no running, jumping jacks, or plyo). Stick strictly to incline walking, stationary bike, and elliptical.

2. Nutrition First (80 / 20)

Weight loss is driven ~80% by sustained caloric deficit. Maintain high protein (1.6–2.0g/kg target weight) to preserve lean muscle.

3. Progressive Form Overload

Use bench box squats to protect knee cartilage. Increase weights gradually and never sacrifice form for heavier load.
