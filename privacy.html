Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our services (such as the OpenFit Protocol). The types of data we collect include:

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your email address and a secure password.
  • Fitness & Telemetry Data: If you use our applications, we store your logged workouts, customized splits, weight, steps, and water intake to provide the tracking service.
  • Payment Information: When you purchase lifetime access or a subscription, your payment is processed securely by Stripe. We never collect, see, or store your credit card numbers on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and personalize the features of our web applications.
  • To securely authenticate your account across your devices.
  • To process your payments and manage your application entitlements.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.

3. Parties to Whom Information is Disclosed

We respect your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to advertisers or data brokers. Your information is only shared with trusted third-party infrastructure providers necessary to run the service:

  • Supabase: Our secure database and authentication provider.
  • Stripe: Our secure payment processor. Stripe uses your data in accordance with their own privacy policies to process transactions and prevent fraud.

4. Security Practices

We take the security of your data extremely seriously. We have implemented the following safeguards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transferred between your browser and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS (HTTPS).
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Our database is mathematically configured to isolate your data. Only your authenticated user session has the cryptographic permission to view or modify your fitness telemetry and app entitlements.
  • Password Protection: We do not store plain-text passwords. All passwords are cryptographically hashed and salted by our authentication provider.

5. Data Deletion & Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data at any time. You can clear your application cache locally or contact our support team to request a complete account deletion from our database servers.
